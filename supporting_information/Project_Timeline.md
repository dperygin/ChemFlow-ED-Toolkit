# ChemFlow-ED: Representative Project Timeline (4-Week Module)

| Week | Milestone | Instructor Checkpoint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Target selection, literature review, structural analysis in PyMOL | Confirm target is appropriate and unliganded; approve biological rationale |
| 2 | Binding pocket identification (DoGSiteScorer); pharmacophore generation and refinement (PharmacoForge); initial virtual screen (Pharmit) | Review pocket justification and pharmacophore rationale before docking begins |
| 3 | Automated molecular docking (batch script); affinity extraction; interaction analysis on top candidates | Spot-check docking setup and interaction-analysis output for correctness |
| 4 | Candidate prioritization; manuscript drafting; oral presentation | Review draft manuscript; grade final deliverables |

## Suggested Instructor Touchpoints

- **Start of Week 1:** Group orientation to the workflow and software
  installation verification (`scripts/verify_installation.py`).
- **End of Week 1:** Individual check-in on target selection and biological
  rationale.
- **End of Week 2:** Individual check-in on pocket and pharmacophore choices
  before committing to a virtual screen.
- **Mid-Week 3:** Troubleshooting session for docking/automation issues.
- **End of Week 4:** Final presentations and manuscript submission.

## Notes for Adaptation

- For shorter modules (e.g., 2 weeks), instructors may supply a preselected
  binding pocket or pharmacophore and have students begin at Stage 4
  (Virtual Screening).
- For graduate courses or extended undergraduate research, Week 4 can be
  followed by additional weeks incorporating molecular dynamics simulation or
  planning for experimental validation.
