# ChemFlow-ED: Example Grading Rubric

Total: 100 points. Point allocations are suggestions and should be adjusted to
institutional grading conventions and course weighting.

| Component | Criteria | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Target Selection & Literature Review** | Biological rationale is sound; target is appropriate for a ligand-independent approach; literature is accurately summarized and cited | 15 |
| **Binding Pocket Identification** | Multiple candidate pockets considered; selection is justified with both computational descriptors and structural reasoning, not druggability score alone | 10 |
| **Pharmacophore Generation** | Initial pharmacophore is chemically reasonable; refinement process and rationale are documented | 15 |
| **Virtual Screening** | Screening strategy and adjustments are documented and justified; hit list is of a reasonable, workable size | 10 |
| **Molecular Docking** | Docking is executed and documented correctly; results are reported completely (poses, affinities) | 10 |
| **Interaction Analysis** | Interaction patterns are correctly interpreted; student distinguishes affinity ranking from structural/interaction evidence | 15 |
| **Candidate Prioritization** | Final shortlist is supported by multiple, explicitly integrated lines of evidence rather than a single score | 10 |
| **Scientific Writing & Communication** | Manuscript follows ACS-style structure; computational predictions are appropriately hedged ("candidate," "predicted," "hypothesized"); figures/tables are clear and correctly captioned | 10 |
| **Oral Presentation** | Clear, well-organized summary of methodology, results, and limitations | 5 |

## Common Deductions

- Treating a favorable docking score as evidence of biological activity
  (rather than a computational prediction requiring experimental validation)
- Selecting the top-ranked binding pocket or docking pose without independent
  structural justification
- Omitting discussion of computational limitations (scoring-function
  approximations, lack of protein flexibility, etc.)
- Incomplete documentation of the iterative refinement process (pharmacophore
  or virtual-screening adjustments)
